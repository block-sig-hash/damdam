## Result

### Previously

An organization could buy for its people (chunk 23) and had no way to see what
that cost, no way to tell service credit from a per-line allowance, and no way
to end somebody's work relationship except by revoking a membership by hand —
which left their invitations live, their funded lines held and their work SIM
active.

### Now

A finance person can see what the organization has and may spend, an
administrator can see what each department used and take an audited copy of it,
and somebody leaving triggers one operation that ends their work access, frees
what was committed for them, asks the carrier to suspend their work lines — and
**leaves their personal service completely alone**.

**API — new `app/enterprise` package (6 endpoints):** funding, departmental
report, audited CSV export, and offboarding (create, list, read).

**Schema — migration `0043_enterprise_offboarding`, two new tables:**
`organization_offboardings`, `offboarding_actions`. Funding and reporting add
**no schema at all**. Documented as [data-model §6.59](../../data-model.md) and
[api-spec §7.42](../../api-spec.md).

**The decisions a reviewer should look at:**

1. **Personal service survives by construction, not by a filter.** Every query
   that finds a person's work lines joins through
   `orders.payer_organization_id`. A line they bought themselves is on an order
   with no organization and is simply not in the result set. The test asserting
   it is the one this chunk exists to pass: the failure mode is somebody losing
   their own phone service on the day they change jobs.
2. **Nothing reassigns a line.** *Do not silently transfer a live profile to
   another person.* An eSIM on a departing employee's handset is not moveable by
   updating a row, so `reassign` is not a method. Reassignment is a suspension
   plus a new line for the new holder.
3. **`pending_carrier` is a state, and it changes the run's outcome.** A run
   with an unanswered suspension is `completed_with_pending`, not `completed`.
   An administrator told "done" about a live line finds out from an invoice.
4. **Every action is recorded, including the ones with nothing to do.** The
   record answers "was their line suspended?" with "they had none" rather than
   with silence.
5. **Offboarding is idempotent across the whole departure**, not merely while a
   run is open. A second request returns the existing record rather than
   re-asking a carrier to suspend a line it is already suspending. A completed
   run only short-circuits when the person is still archived — if they were
   re-added since, this is a new departure.
6. **Service credit, per-line allowance and supplier pooling are three
   different things**, and `pooling` is a field that says `false` rather than a
   silence a dashboard could fill. No adapter advertises pooling; chunk 16 found
   no evidence for it.
7. **`period_cap: null` means undecided.** Not unlimited, not zero. A client
   rendering a missing cap as infinity is inventing a commitment.
8. **A report carries conservative freshness.** `observed_through` is the
   oldest per-line watermark; it is null if any work line has never been
   observed. One healthy line cannot hide a stalled one.
9. **Purchases and usage are never summed.** One is known when an order is
   placed, the other when a supplier says so.
10. **Reading a departure is `people:read`, performing one is `member:revoke`.**
    The write inherits chunk 07's step-up requirement; the read does not,
    because requiring a second factor to view a list nobody can act on trains
    administrators to step up for everything.

## Known gaps and handoff

- **Carrier suspension is a request, not an act.** Everything downstream of
  `SUSPEND_LINE` is pending until an adapter exists and something calls
  `confirm_action`. The state machine is complete and the confirmation entry
  point is tested; what is missing is the supplier and the worker that polls it.
- **No invoice is issued.** `financial_documents` models one and this chunk
  reports what an invoice would contain without producing a numbered document.
  Issuing one needs a seller entity (D3, open) and a tax treatment (also open) —
  inventing either would put a legal entity and a tax line on a customer's
  invoice that nobody selected.
- **Paid or supplier-uncertain top-ups are not silently reversed.** Requested
  and reserved organization top-ups are cancelled and their holds released.
  Paid top-ups surface an exception pending refund policy; provisioning/unknown
  supplier outcomes stay held and pending reconciliation.
- **Reporting is not paginated.** A report iterates an organization's people and
  their lines per department. Correct at the size this product sells today and
  not at ten thousand staff; the shape to fix it is an aggregate query per
  department rather than a loop, and it is a rewrite worth doing when somebody
  has that many people rather than speculatively.
- **No deployment, store submission or production deletion was performed**, and
  none is authorized by this assignment.
