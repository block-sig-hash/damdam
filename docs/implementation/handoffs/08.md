### Design decisions a later chunk must reuse

1. **A status pill declares which question it answers.** `data-model.md` §6.44
   split payment, provisioning, installation, activation and network attachment
   apart because they genuinely disagree — a profile can sit installed on a
   phone that never attaches. The `family` prop is required, with no default: a
   pill whose meaning depends on where you found it is a pill that will
   eventually be found somewhere else.
2. **A usage reading is never shown without its age.** Carrier usage arrives
   late; a balance with no timestamp is a number people plan around, and that is
   how somebody runs out mid-journey while the app says they had plenty. "Never
   reported" renders as an **empty grey bar**, not a full green one — painting
   it full would be inventing a reading.
3. **Empty, pending, blocked and failed stay visually distinct.** An empty list
   is not a problem; rendering it in the same red as a failed payment teaches
   people to ignore the colour. `blocked` exists separately from `error` because
   "your phone cannot use an eSIM" is not something retrying fixes.
4. **Money is addressed explicitly.** Every failure says whether anything was
   charged; every post-payment retry says whether it charges again. With money
   already taken, that is the only question anybody has.
5. **`pending` is a sentence, not a spinner.** Provisioning takes minutes, and a
   four-minute spinner reads as a hang.

### Three defects the work surfaced

1. **`accent-500` fails contrast as a foreground colour.** Computing the ratios
   rather than restating them showed Desert Gold is **2.97:1** on white — under
   even the 3:1 non-text floor. §1 described it as a badge fill and never
   claimed a ratio, but nothing stopped it being used as an icon or a border,
   and it reads as "dark enough" by eye. Now formally fill-only, enforced by
   test. Several ratios I initially guessed for the two new colours were also
   wrong, which is the argument for computing them.
2. **Both state components doubled a full stop** when joining already-punctuated
   translated copy into an accessibility label — "38 lines are ready.. Retrying
   does not charge you again." — in every locale simultaneously. Fixed once in a
   shared `announce()` helper with its own tests.
3. **Two eSIM flows are platform-tagged.** Writing the matrix manifest showed
   that claiming them for both platforms would leave the other platform's matrix
   permanently and misleadingly incomplete. The manifest now carries a platform
   list per group.

   clear 4.5:1 while staying recognisably in family. Both are load-bearing for
   §17.2's argument, and both are worth a designer's eye rather than only a
   contrast check.
