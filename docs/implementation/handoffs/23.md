## Result

### Previously

An organization could describe its people (chunk 22) and nothing else. Buying
for them meant fifty consumer checkouts, one per person, with no shared
progress, no per-line funding, no way to resume a half-finished run and no way
to hand a line to somebody who has no account with us.

### Now

An administrator selects people, sees which of them cannot be served and why,
funds one hold per line, provisions them independently, resumes what failed
without ever retrying what was merely *lost*, cancels individual lines, and
hands each recipient a single-use invitation to claim their own.

**API — new `app/bulk` package (9 endpoints).** Eight under
`/v1/organizations/{id}` for the buyer; one under `/v1/me` for the recipient.

**Schema — migration `0042_bulk_provisioning`, three new tables:** `bulk_jobs`,
`bulk_job_items`, `activation_requests`. Additive; `orders`, `order_items` and
`ledger_reservations` are referenced and otherwise untouched. Documented as
[data-model §6.58](../../data-model.md) and [api-spec §7.41](../../api-spec.md).

**Refactor within scope:** `CatalogService.sale_terms` — a public lookup for
"who sells this here, and for how much", so a bulk order and a consumer quote
cannot drift into two answers about the same product on the same day.

**The decisions a reviewer should look at:**

1. **A bulk order is fifty independent purchases.** The items carry the state;
   the job carries counts written in the same transaction as the item they
   describe. "Forty-seven provisioned, two unknown, one invalid" is expressible,
   and a job that rounded that to "failed" would misreport forty-seven working
   lines.
2. **One hold per line, and a retry takes a *fresh* one.** This is the defect
   the fifty-recipient scenario caught during development: `mark_failed`
   releases the hold, so a retry that reused `reservation_id` would provision a
   line the organization is not holding money for. `_ensure_hold` is the single
   place that decides a line is funded, and an outstanding balance is the only
   thing that counts.
3. **`unknown` is not reachable from the retry path.** `resumable` returns two
   lists and an unknown item is only ever in the first. A caller that ignored
   reconciliation entirely still could not buy a second profile for that
   recipient — not unlikely, *not possible*.
4. **Silence is not permission.** A reconciler that returns `None` leaves the
   item unknown with its hold intact, rather than assuming either outcome.
5. **`provision` is idempotent.** A worker that lost the response and retried is
   asking a question, not making a second purchase, so a finished job answers
   with its progress instead of a 409.
6. **A provisioned line is not cancelled by releasing its hold.** Releasing a
   reservation for a service the customer has is giving value away. Actual
   settlement and a captured payment to refund are not present in this path, so
   the provisioned-line refund remains downstream work. An `unknown` line is
   refused too — the supplier may have provisioned it.
7. **Recipients are not users.** `order_items.recipient_user_id` stays null
   until an activation request is redeemed. Inventing an account at purchase
   would bind a line to an identity nobody authenticated.
8. **Redeeming is not an administrator action.** The recipient endpoint takes an
   ordinary session and no organization permission. Requiring tenant membership
   to accept a work SIM would be exactly backwards.
9. **A request is not an installation.** `activation_requests` records the
   invitation and its redemption only. Installation is chunk 15's
   `esim_installations`; attachment is `carrier_lines`; the view carries
   neither.
10. **Internet-only assignment buys no carrier profile.** A `VOICE` product takes
    the entitlement path and stops, per the calling amendment.

## Known gaps and handoff

- **No approved carrier adapter or reconciler is wired.** Chunk 20 is merged and
  the runtime now shares its `ConnectivityService`, so an internet-only product
  receives its local entitlement. The runtime deliberately does not assert a
  carrier confirmation: a carrier line is funded and ordered and then **stays
  `ordered`**. Service tests inject `carrier_provisioning_confirmed=True` to
  exercise a confirmed outcome; that fixture is not a supplier call.
- **Nothing sends the invitation.** `issue_activation_request` returns the token
  to the caller; no email or SMS is dispatched. The notification path exists
  (chunk 21's preferences, the notification service) and wiring it means
  deciding what a work-line invitation says and in whose name — a product
  question, not a plumbing one.
- **`resume` has no reconciler in the HTTP path.** The route calls `resume`
  without one, so an unknown item stays unknown and visible. That is correct and
  incomplete: reconciliation talks to a supplier, and there is no supplier.
- **Partial refund is refused, not performed.** Cancelling a provisioned line
  returns 409. This path creates reservations and an authorized order, but no
  captured `PaymentAttempt`; an actual refund requires the later settlement and
  refund-policy integration.
- **A job's counters are maintained by the service, not by the database.** Job
  mutations now lock the row and write counters with their item, so normal
  concurrent API operation is serialized. A direct SQL update can still
  desynchronise the cache; there is no trigger-derived invariant.
- **No deployment, store submission or production deletion was performed.**
