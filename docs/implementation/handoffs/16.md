## The seven things worth reviewing closely

### 1. A cumulative counter is not a delta

`current_billing_period_consumed_data` is cumulative and it resets. Reading each
poll as consumption charges the whole cycle again every time — a customer on a
five-minute poller would exhaust a 5 GB plan within the hour.

`ingest_counter` stores the raw reading, computes the delta against the previous
one, and writes nothing at all when the counter has not moved. A poll finding
nothing new is the common case; writing a zero-quantity row every minute would
bury the real records and make `occurred_to` claim an observation where nothing
was measured.

**A decrease is a reset, never negative usage.** Telnyx exposes no billing-cycle
boundary, so the two cannot be told apart — the conservative reading keeps prior
consumption, treats the new value as a fresh cycle, and raises a
`usage_counter_reset` exception so somebody checks it against supplier billing.
Recording a negative delta would credit bytes the customer really used.

### 2. Deduplication, and an honest note about its limits

**Telnyx publishes no WDR record id.** The OpenAPI source documents the report
envelope and not the record, and the prose field list names neither a byte count
nor an id. So `natural_key` falls back to a composite of the documented fields.

That composite is imperfect and the docstring says so: two genuinely distinct
sessions with identical boundaries and identical volume collapse into one. It
under-counts, in the customer's favour, which is the right direction to be wrong
when a supplier will not give us an id. Codex may disagree with that trade; it is
recorded rather than hidden so the disagreement can happen.

Redelivery is **idempotent, not an error**. Raising on a replayed page would turn
every supplier retry into an incident.

**Late and out-of-order records are counted.** Nothing in ingestion compares an
event against a watermark: a watermark is about coverage, not about whether a
record is welcome, and rejecting a late CDR is how usage goes missing. A two-day
-old session also does not make the balance's observation time older — that
tracks the newest usage, not the newest arrival.

### 3. One authoritative data source per line

A supplier can report the same megabyte twice. Counting both bills a customer
twice for one download, which is the "avoid charging the same data via two
sources" rule in `AGENTS.md`.

`carrier_lines.authoritative_data_source` decides; the other source's records are
`EVIDENCE` — kept, compared, never charged. Kept rather than dropped because it
is the only way a disagreement with supplier billing is ever found:
`compare_sources` raises an exception and **adjusts nothing**, because which
figure is right is a conversation with the supplier.

Voice is exempt: there is only ever one voice source, and forcing it through the
data source's choice would silence a whole stream because a counter arrived
first.

### 4. Corrections converge without double-counting

A supplier restating a session writes a **new** record; the original becomes
`SUPERSEDED` and keeps its numbers, so "what were they told last week" stays
answerable. Allowance sums exclude `SUPERSEDED` and `EVIDENCE`, so the balance
lands on the corrected figure and was never the sum of both.

The money follows chunk 10's discipline: a correction posts a compensating entry
for the **difference** under its own business event.
`test_a_correction_posts_only_the_difference` asserts the second entry moves 25,
not 50 — reversing and re-posting would put two transactions in the books where
one adjustment happened.

`ck_usage_records_corrections_are_derived` means a supplier feed cannot rewrite
history by asserting a `corrects_id`.

### 5. Carrier and internet usage are separate facts

The calling amendment requires internet calling not to depend on a carrier line
foreign key, and says outright that *"WebRTC CDRs cannot prove native-carrier
usage or spending caps"*.

`usage_records.channel` plus a nullable `carrier_line_id` and
`ck_usage_records_channel_line`: a carrier record names its line, an internet
record must not. A WebRTC record therefore has no line to be summed against —
structural, rather than a query that remembers to exclude it.

**Chunk 16 ingests only the carrier channel.** V03 owns internet metering. The
column exists so V03 does not have to migrate this table, which is the
"coordinate usage provenance and ledger interfaces with V03" the amendment asks
of this chunk.

### 6. A number nobody measured is not a measurement

`allowance()` returns `observed_at` and a freshness of `fresh`, `stale` or
`unknown`. **`unknown` is not a degraded `stale`**: stale means we measured and
it is old; unknown means nobody has ever measured and the only number available
is the grant. That is the assignment's "refreshing an initialized database
balance is not reconciliation", made visible to whatever renders it.

`has_provisional` says the figure may still be revised, so a counter-derived
estimate is not shown as a settled bill. Remaining is floored at zero — a
negative reads as a credit in a UI.

### 7. Cursors are watermarks

`position_at` means "we have everything before this", so it never moves
backwards and never moves while a backfill is open. A poller stopped for hours
**backfills** rather than restarting from now; restarting from now looks healthy
immediately and silently loses every byte in the gap. Closing a backfill moves
the watermark to the **window's end**, not to now: time after the window still
has not been covered.

Five consecutive failures stall the cursor and raise
`usage_polling_stalled`. A silently stalled usage poller is invisible from
outside — the balance simply stops moving.

## Decision gates

**D1 is OPEN.** No supplier usage has ever been observed:

- `USAGE_EVENTS` is a **withheld** Telnyx capability, with the reason recorded:
  no documented record id means no deduplication from documented fields alone.
- `fetch_usage_report` returns the report's state and window and **does not
  parse the report file**. Its schema is not published; writing a parser against
  a prose bullet list and calling it an integration is exactly the invented
  contract `AGENTS.md` forbids.
- The staleness threshold (15 minutes) is a **product decision, not a supplier
  fact** — Telnyx documents no usage latency. It is a constructor argument.
- API-CONTRACTS.md §4.5 questions 6 and 8 are the ones that unblock this: do
  WDRs carry a stable id and can they be restated, and is the WDR byte count the
  same measurement as the cumulative counter.

**D5 (charging policy) is OPEN.** This chunk computes and posts what usage
costs, using the pinned tariff version. It does **not** decide what happens when
an allowance runs out — whether the customer is charged beyond it, cut off, or
neither. Chunk 17 owns enforcement; the policy is D5's.

## Migrations and compatibility

- **Fresh install:** `alembic upgrade head` from empty — pass.
- **Old-data upgrade:** the one added column is nullable and unset; no backfill.
  Setting `authoritative_data_source` for existing lines would be choosing a

  `exception_items` row is rewritten and no exclusive lock is taken on a table

  rewrite every row for no benefit.

## Known gaps and handoff

- **No worker or schedule is wired.** `UsageService` is driven by a caller.
  Wiring the polling loop to chunk 11's outbox is chunk 25/26 work; the cursor
  semantics it depends on are documented at the method.
- **The WDR file is not downloaded or parsed.** That is the whole of the
  event-stream path, and it needs a real, observed file. Until then the counter
  is the only ingestible source, which is why `USAGE_COUNTER` is advertised and
  `USAGE_EVENTS` is not.
- **`settle_charge` needs its accounts supplied by the caller.** Which ledger
  accounts a usage charge moves between is a chunk 17 / D5 question, so this
  chunk takes them as arguments rather than choosing.
- **Voice allowance and voice money are both tracked and not reconciled with
  each other.** A call consumes `voice_seconds` *and* computes a tariff amount.
  Whether a customer inside their allowance is also charged the tariff is D5's
  to answer; today both numbers are recorded and nothing decides.
- **For Codex:** the natural-key trade in §2 is the judgement call most worth a
  second opinion. The alternative — refusing to ingest events without a supplier
  id at all — is defensible and stricter, and would make the WDR path
  unavailable rather than approximate.
- No merge, deployment, vendor message or live call has been performed.
