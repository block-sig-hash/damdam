## Findings and fixes

1. **P1 — unclaimed bulk recipients vanished from reports and offboarding.**
   Both paths joined only through a claimed `user_id`, although chunk 23 keeps
   that null until redemption. They now also join through
   `bulk_job_items.person_id`, so pre-claim purchases, entitlements and carrier
   lines remain attributable to the intended employee.

2. **P1 — local internet-only service survived employment termination.**
   Offboarding looked only for carrier lines. It now expires organization-paid
   local entitlements immediately and records that separately from the carrier
   suspension that may remain pending. Personal entitlements are untouched.

3. **P1 — ordered or unknown supplier work could be silently omitted.** A
   runtime carrier item can legitimately remain ordered with its hold intact.
   Offboarding now records such line cancellation as `pending_carrier` and does
   not release money until the supplier outcome is reconciled.

4. **P1 — the chunk bypassed the accepted membership lifecycle.** Direct row
   mutation skipped the last-owner invariant, MFA-elevation revocation and
   calling revocation listeners. A dedicated offboarding transition now reuses
   all three; the last owner is refused and organization-funded active calls go
   through the accepted V02/V03 stop/hangup path.

5. **P1 — concurrent offboarding could race through the idempotency read.** The
   recipient row is locked before checking prior runs. A two-session PostgreSQL
   regression proves concurrent requests converge on one offboarding record.
   Activation-request issuance takes the same lock first and refuses archived
   recipients, closing the inverse race that could create an invitation after
   departure.

6. **P1 — pending organization top-ups were declared but not handled.** Safe
   requested/reserved top-ups are failed and their holds released. Settled
   top-ups surface an exception pending an explicit refund; provisioning or
   outcome-unknown supplier work remains held and pending reconciliation.

7. **P1 — carrier confirmation did not update the carrier line.** Confirming a
   suspension action now transitions the referenced line to suspended and
   refuses confirmation of actions that are not pending. Failed local actions
   yield `completed_with_exceptions`, not a misleading completed state.

8. **P2 — one current line could hide every stalled line in report freshness.**
   `observed_through` used the organization's maximum usage timestamp. It is now
   the minimum of each work entitlement's latest authoritative observation and
   becomes null when any line has never been observed.

9. **P2 — migration and documentation identities collided with accepted work.**
   The integrated migration follows `0042_bulk_provisioning` as
   `0043_enterprise_offboarding`; its amendments are data-model §6.59 and API
   §7.42.

10. **P1 — authorized reservations were reported as settled spend.** The
    funding view now separates `committed_this_period` (authorized or paid
    orders) from `spent_this_period` (paid only). Policy headroom subtracts
    commitments, while an authorized bulk order is no longer described as a
    charge.

## Remaining gates

- The funding endpoint is a read model over existing service credit and policy;
  it does not collect enterprise funds. D3/D4/D5 still block a live merchant,
  seller/tax treatment and approved enterprise funding terms.
- No invoice is issued. A legal seller and tax treatment remain open, so a
  numbered financial document cannot be truthfully generated yet.
- Carrier suspension and supplier reconciliation have durable pending states,
  but no approved carrier adapter/worker confirms them. No live suspension is
  claimed.
- Paid top-ups and provisioned services require explicit refund policy and a
  captured/settled payment path. They are surfaced, not automatically reversed.
- No enterprise dashboard is included, and reporting currently iterates people
  and their allocations rather than using a large-tenant aggregate query.
