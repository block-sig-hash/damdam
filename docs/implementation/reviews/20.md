## Findings and fixes

1. **P1 — a delivery grant was not bound to the addressed line.** A holder of
   two lines could present line A's token to line B's route, consume A's
   one-time profile and receive it under B's response. Redemption now requires
   the expected credential id inside the locked vault transaction and returns
   the same generic refusal for foreign, spent and unknown grants.

2. **P1 — concurrent deliveries could overwrite the credential counter.** A
   session that had cached the credential before another redemption could write
   a stale count. Redemption now reloads and locks both the grant and credential;
   the PostgreSQL regression proves two pre-issued grants advance the count to
   two without a lost update.

3. **P1 — activation material could outlive its protected screen.** Reveal was
   available before screen protection resolved, and late reads or redemptions
   could populate a screen after navigation, reopening or backgrounding. The
   flow now gates reveal on the protection result, invalidates stale generations,
   clears the credential when inactive, and releases protection on every exit.
   Unexpected native errors use first-party copy because provider text may echo
   the activation code.

4. **P1 — My Line recovery could show the wrong line or a false ready state.**
   Consuming an initial navigation intent restarted the list flow, late reads
   could overwrite a newer selection, and an absent installation/carrier record
   could be presented as ready. Navigation intent and request generations are
   now stable, while missing provider state remains an explicit wait.

5. **P1 — carrier rates mixed incompatible tariff rows.** My Line returned
   internet-origin rates beside carrier rates and omitted roaming origin,
   destination kind, minimum and setup context. The API now selects the origin
   mode that matches delivery and preserves the full rate context; the mobile
   formatter narrows currency safely and renders the same facts in both locales.

6. **P1 — several customer statements exceeded the evidence.** The submitted
   screen equated active records with a working line, said every suspension
   clears by topping up, implied unavailable internet calling fell back to the
   native dialer, and prescribed buying again for device replacement. The copy
   now separates installation, activation and attachment; directs unexplained
   suspension and replacement to support; and describes internet calling as
   unavailable without inventing a fallback.

7. **P2 — the required QR fallback and iOS snapshot protection were absent.**
   The app now encodes the LPA locally into SVG with `qrcode-generator`, retains
   manual fields if encoding fails, and makes no external QR request. iOS covers
   the window synchronously before an app-switcher snapshot and removes the
   cover on return. Android treats a native `false` response as protection
   failure rather than success.

8. **P2 — the screenshot flows did not reach the new targets.** The six My Line
   flows now scroll their target into view in both locales and are registered in
   the matrix tests. One existing PurchaseFlow test received a 15-second timeout
   after the same renderer-contention timeout recurred in CI while passing
   locally; no assertion or production behavior was weakened.
