# DamDam payment-provider eligibility enquiry

Draft for review; not sent. Prepared 7 September 2026.

Subject: Isle of Man merchant eligibility — eSIM mobile voice and data service

Hello,

We are developing DamDam, a mobile connectivity service for individual
customers, enterprises, and government agencies. We intend to sell eSIM plans
providing carrier mobile voice and data, with Telnyx selected as our intended
launch supplier. Supplier coverage and commercial onboarding are still being
validated. Customers will purchase plans and top-ups; organizations will also
provision and fund employee services through a dashboard.

We are considering an Isle of Man trading company and want to establish
payment-processing eligibility before committing to an integration.

Could you confirm:

1. Can you onboard an Isle of Man company for this business model, including
   prepaid telecom plans, data top-ups, and any service credit? Please identify
   any distinction between direct merchant onboarding and platform submerchants.
2. What operating-presence, director/beneficial-owner residence, incorporation,
   banking, and supplier-contract information would you require?
3. Do you accept newly launched merchants, and what projected volume, minimum
   invoice or monthly commitment applies?
4. Which customer countries, card brands, Apple Pay/Google Pay, payment
   currencies and settlement currencies can this entity use? Please include
   acceptance of Nigerian-issued cards and any relevant cross-border limits.
5. What are the processing, international-card, FX, refund and chargeback fees,
   payout schedule, reserve/hold requirements and settlement-bank criteria?
6. Can we support saved payment methods, customer-authorized auto top-up or
   subscription renewal, and enterprise purchases with invoice references?
7. What evidence is needed for provisioning failures, partial refunds, and
   disputes involving already activated or partially consumed telecom service?

Please provide an initial eligibility assessment and the documents required
for formal underwriting. We can then provide our proposed markets, average
order value, projected processing volumes, ownership and operating details.

Thank you.
